Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 26, 2026 • ORIVIA Demonstration Platform

1. Overview & Demonstration Nature

ORIVIA is a demonstration travel platform designed to showcase modern web architecture, third-party AI chatbot integration, and automated security auditing. This policy outlines how information submitted through the website, contact inquiries, and interactive chat tools is handled.

2. Information Collected via First-Party Contact Form

When you submit an inquiry through /contact.html, our first-party Cloudflare Worker API processes:

  • Your submitted name, email address, and itinerary vision.
  • Security challenge telemetry (via Cloudflare Turnstile) to verify human submission.
  • Client IP addresses processed ephemerally for rate-limiting and anti-abuse verification.

3. Third-Party AI Chatbot Processing (Botpress)

The interactive conversational assistant available on this site is provided by Botpress Cloud (Botpress Inc.). When engaging with the chatbot:

  • Conversation messages are transmitted directly from your browser to Botpress servers (*.botpress.cloud).
  • Data submitted to the chat is governed by Botpress's Privacy Policy.
  • Strict Warning: Do not submit passwords, payment card details, bank credentials, or sensitive personal data into the chat window.

4. Security & Anti-Abuse (Cloudflare)

To safeguard our infrastructure, this platform utilizes Cloudflare Pages, Cloudflare Turnstile, and Cloudflare Bot Fight Mode. Cloudflare may process network-level telemetry and browser characteristics solely to detect malicious automated crawlers and mitigate distributed denial-of-service (DDoS) events.

5. Data Retention & User Rights

Because ORIVIA is a demonstration platform, inquiry records are kept for ephemeral validation testing only and are pruned periodically. You may request the deletion of any submitted contact record by emailing privacy@orivia-demo.pages.dev.
