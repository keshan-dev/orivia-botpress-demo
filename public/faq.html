Official Atelier Policies

The definitive source of truth regarding ORIVIA bookings, pricing structures, and conversational AI boundaries.

Strict Policy: No. ORIVIA does not offer instant online checkouts, credit card charging, or automated reservations. Because every journey requires customized air logistics, estate buyouts, and security clearance, all reservations must be originated through our formal inquiry process and confirmed directly by a human concierge director.
ORIVIA does not publish fixed prices. Each expedition is bespoke. Pricing is influenced by travel party size, seasonal availability, private aircraft positioning, and custom estate staffing. Any estimate given by our conversational AI assistant is purely illustrative; exact financial terms are provided solely in written client proposals.
The ORIVIA digital assistant is powered by Botpress Cloud. It serves exclusively as an informational exploration guide. It does NOT possess access to private customer accounts, cannot verify bank details, cannot process payments, and cannot finalize travel bookings.
